# DRM development status

**Updated:** 2 November 2026. Public preparation edition; not a released standard.

Phases 1–3 have completed their documented development work. The AMRKB and OSDS
bounded documentary preservation pilots were separately accepted and Phase 4 is
closed within those scopes. **Phase 5 is not authorised; Phases 5–8 remain future
work.** The [eight-phase roadmap](DRM_v1_Roadmap.md) is the sole development
sequence, distinct from the iterative A–F database methodology.

DRM-MSP 0.1.0 review revision 2 remains a **candidate**, not generally adopted or
released. Specific approved methodological principles and remaining proposals
are distinguished in the [decision summary](DECISIONS.md). No new methodological
approval or protocol change results from public-edition cleanup.

Available: current methodological documentation, candidate protocol, empty
synthetic template, 48 synthetic fixtures, expected outcomes and standard-library
structural checks. No operational register, real-data adapter, federation service,
full conformance validator or verified external connector is established here.
These synthetic checks do not reproduce private pilots or historical prototypes.

Historical plans, transcripts, real research evidence and detailed execution
records are excluded. Public documentation and tests are self-contained; no
private archive or source database is needed. See [provenance and limitations](PROVENANCE.md)
and the [synthetic checks guide](examples/msp/README.md).

No repository-wide licence or publication permission is implied. Attribution,
publication rights and code/documentation licensing require owner confirmation
before publication. No Git repository has been initialised in this preparation
folder. First commit and publication remain separate owner decisions.
