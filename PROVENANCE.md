# Public-edition provenance and evidence limits

**Preparation date:** 1 November 2026. This is a curated derivative of the project's
current methodological documents and synthetic tooling, not a verbatim research
archive, released DRM specification or permission to publish.

## Derivation and authority

The approved architecture separates methodology, federation infrastructure,
autonomous domain research and IRKB synthesis. The current eight-phase roadmap
replaces an earlier planning sequence. Specific methodological decisions are
summarised in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md); unapproved detailed proposals remain
identified in the methodological framework and MSP candidate.

A private development archive retains the original documents, source editions,
requirement-level crosswalks, approval history, pilot evidence and a mapping of
original/public file hashes and editorial transformations. Those materials are
not dependencies of this public-facing checkout and are not redistributed here.
Authorised evidence review remains possible under separately agreed access; public
synthetic tests cannot independently establish private pilot findings.

The historical v0.1.0 core was recovered as a proposed draft. Later domain-profile,
interoperability, prototype and preliminary repository-validation packages remain
missing or incompletely evidenced. A reported twelve-test synthetic prototype was
not reproduced. One earlier contextual README edition also remains unavailable;
no original was reconstructed or substituted. Private completeness limitations
are not resolved by this publication preparation.

## Phase 4 acceptance and limits

Two bounded documentary preservation pilots, AMRKB and OSDS, were separately
accepted within their documented scopes and Phase 4 closed. Their private evidence
remains excluded. This edition does not redistribute source research, quotations,
source editions, manifests, full pilot logs or controlled excerpts. Acceptance is
not scientific verification, identity equivalence, general MSP adoption or rights
to redistribute external material. Proposed pilot clarifications remain proposals.
Phase 5 is not authorised.

## Publicly reproducible synthetic checks

The examples are newly prepared synthetic declarations. Both checker modules,
the empty template, 48 fixture files, expected outcomes and a synthetic file-evidence
sample are included. They need no source database or private archive at runtime.

The public legacy-regression check pins the bytes of eighteen authorised synthetic
fixtures using SHA-256 values in the public legacy oracle, then checks their
expected outcomes. These hashes were verified against the preserved earlier
synthetic edition during preparation. This replaces runtime comparison with the
private snapshot. It retains legacy byte/outcome checks, but does not prove the
private snapshot's custody or reproduce its wider historical context.

Other checks remain separately labelled legacy or review revision 2. A successful
structure may carry unresolved identity, rights or disagreement limitations.
Neither a hash nor a declared role authenticates the scientific or human claim.

## Attribution, rights and publication status

Professional project attribution is retained in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff).

as fixture evidence. Standards and systems are named as references or planned
integration targets, not claims of adopted standards, endorsement or conformance.

No repository-wide licence has been selected. Owner confirmation of public
