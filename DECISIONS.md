# DRM public decision and review summary

**Edition:** 1 November 2026. This summary preserves methodological boundaries and
current status; it is not the private approval transcript or a conformance award.

## Approved architecture

- DRM is methodology-first: heterogeneous database design, evaluation, governance
  and interoperability, supported by bounded reference software.
- Preserve domain authority, legitimate overlap, evidence, rights, identity and
  scientific/technical limits. Do not impose a universal scientific ontology.
- Separate shared infrastructure from source assertions and IRKB research.
  Applications can access sources directly.
- Retain twelve principles and iterative A–F database methodology, distinct from
  the eight DRM development phases.
- Keep accountable core requirements, recommended patterns and optional extensions
  distinct. Preserve ingestion and planned Zotero/Zenodo metadata demonstrations.
- Complete federation migration and completed IRKB research are not prerequisites
  for DRM v1.0. IRKB can develop concepts against versioned provisional contracts.

## Specifically approved methodological basis

1. Three obligation levels, justified applicability and proportionate evidence;
   an unmet mandatory requirement remains a limitation, not a full pass.
2. Methodological exceptions require DRM-owner review; scientific acceptance and
   rights authority remain with the responsible domains/custodians.
3. Structural exchange, domain semantic review and source/IRKB assertion authorship
   are separate. Contested alignments stay visible and use approval is scoped.
4. Compatibility depends on effects. Invalidating prior conformance requires a
   breaking-version path or explicit opt-in profile, not a MINOR label alone.
5. Ten-topic profile accountability and selective boundary criteria without
   universal scoring; historical evidence limits remain accepted limitations.

These specific approvals do not adopt every historical recommendation or every
detailed methodological proposal. The framework retains review qualifications.

## Candidate protocol and completed review boundary

DRM-MSP 0.1.0 review revision 2 was permitted as a provisional bounded pilot basis,
not generally adopted or released. Its seven subjects and example format are
separate concepts. Field names and role combinations in examples are illustrative.

The AMRKB bounded documentary preservation pilot was accepted separately within
its documented scope. The OSDS bounded documentary preservation pilot was also
accepted separately within its documented scope. Phase 4 is closed. This accepts
preservation findings and disclosed limits, not scientific truth, external identity
equivalence, unrestricted rights or full MSP/DRM conformance.

## Proposed clarifications and future decisions

Four pilot recommendations remain proposals: proportionate preservation evidence;
separate source/proposal/scientific/permission states; explicit checker limits;
and later domain-authorised typing/identity review. No protocol change follows
automatically from accepting the reports.

Remaining decisions include exact conformance applicability, standards fitness,
exchange/registry contracts, implementation choices, external integrations,
compatibility guarantees and future release/rights/licensing. Historical test
families remain distinct until reconciled with evidence. No historical prototype
has been verified by the current synthetic examples.

**Phase 5 and Phases 6–8 remain future work. Phase 5 is not authorised.**
Publication, Git operations, MSP adoption and Phase 5 require separate decisions.
